# Play one real dinner party, with real children

## Next step

Run `node prototypes/dinner-party/server.mjs`, put it on a laptop everyone can reach on the
local network, and play one Sleeping Beauty with actual guests — at least one child under
seven, at least six adults. Do not build anything first.

Watch for four things, in this order of interest:

1. **Does a child do the pretending unprompted**, or does it only happen because a grown-up
   asks for the clue? The whole design assumes the favour is the fun part rather than a toll.
2. **Does anybody read the encounter lists out loud?** That is the only route to catching the
   curser, and nothing in the UI teaches it.
3. **Do five candles and ninety seconds on the floor feel right?** Every number in this is a
   guess.
4. **Is two voices for a duo too many, or not enough to notice?**

## What we know

The machine works and the puzzle is fair — 3600 parties solvable, 2000 with falsehoods
planted and none silently convicting an innocent, twenty guests seated in under a minute
without accounts. None of that says whether an evening of it is fun, and no further
simulation will.
